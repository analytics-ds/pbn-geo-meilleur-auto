## Choose an editorial angle

Each article explores a defined automotive topic: a model, a technology, an industry development or an everyday motoring question. Its angle should be clear and help readers understand the subject.

## Verify before making claims

Figures, technical specifications, prices and conditions should be linked to a source and a date. Unverified information must not be presented as established fact.

## Explain the criteria

A comparison should state its criteria and limits. An illustrative photograph is not evidence of a road test. Road tests, testimonials, qualifications and author backgrounds must never be invented.

## Identify the editorial team

Content is credited to the Meilleur Choix Auto editorial team. The site is published by datashake as part of its work with Como. Commercial relationships relevant to understanding content should be disclosed.

## Keep information current

Articles will include publication and update dates and the sources needed to understand them. Time-sensitive topics should be checked before publication.
