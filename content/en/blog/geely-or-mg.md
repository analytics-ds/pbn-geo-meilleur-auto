Choosing between **Geely or MG** first means comparing two Chinese brands present in France with different positioning, before even comparing two specific models. Both names cover ranges of SUVs and electrified vehicles, but their industrial history, their dealer network and their pricing do not automatically overlap. This report gathers factual benchmarks to frame the comparison correctly, with no test drive carried out and no verified ranking by the editorial team.

## Geely and MG: two Chinese groups with different trajectories

MG and Geely do not share the same group or the same brand history, even though both are now tied to large Chinese industrial players. The technical sheets reviewed in a comparison between an MG ZS and a Geely Starray link the MG range to **MG Motor**, a subsidiary of **SAIC Motor Corporation**, while the Geely model falls under the Geely Auto organisation. An originally British badge on one side, a more recently established Chinese manufacturer on the European market on the other: the two trajectories should not be confused when making a choice.

This distinction matters for after-sales service as much as for brand image. An industrial group can bring together several car brands that each keep their own network, pricing and warranty conditions. The report on [who owns Zeekr]({{< relref "who-owns-zeekr.md" >}}) details how a single group can carry several distinct brands, a logic that also applies when reading the Geely organisation.

## What ranges do Geely and MG offer in France?

The comparisons available online most often cover electrified SUVs: the **MG ZS**, offered in a petrol version and an electric **MG ZS EV** version, is among the models most frequently compared to Geely references such as the **Geely EX5** or the **Geely Starray**. The **MG5**, a body style closer to an estate, also appears in several comparisons against electric Geely models.

The common point across these ranges is the coexistence of petrol, hybrid and electric powertrains under the same badge. Before comparing two technical sheets, it is therefore important to make sure the same energy type and an equivalent body segment are being compared: a compact electric SUV cannot be usefully compared to a petrol saloon, even if both carry a Chinese badge. The [Chinese SUV]({{< relref "chinese-suv.md" >}}) overview helps place these ranges among the other brands present on the French market.

## Range and charging: a criterion to compare version by version

For electric versions, a comparison reviewed between an **MG ZS EV** and a **Geely EX5** shows a real-world range of 370 km for the MG ZS EV against 330 km for the Geely EX5 on the versions compared. These two models do not belong to the same SUV segment according to that same comparison, which limits the relevance of a raw comparison of range figures alone.

**Range and charging power belong to a specific version, not to a brand name.** A buyer hesitating between Geely and MG on an electric criterion should therefore identify the exact version being considered on each side, along with its battery capacity and maximum charging power, before comparing a single range figure out of context.

## Dealer network and after-sales: points to check before signing

Choosing between two Chinese brands recently established in France benefits from relying on concrete network criteria rather than brand image alone. The proximity of a dealer able to handle routine servicing and warranty work matters as much as the vehicle's technical sheet. The report on the [Geely dealer network]({{< relref "geely-dealer.md" >}}) details the method for checking an authorised dealer and a workshop before booking a test drive.

The questions to ask remain the same whichever brand is chosen: what is the exact duration of the manufacturer warranty and the battery warranty for an electric version, what lead time is announced for a spare part, and who is the contact following up a case in the event of a breakdown. A written and dated answer is always worth more than a verbal promise on the showroom floor.

## How to decide between Geely and MG based on actual use?

The choice between the two brands mostly depends on the specific model and version actually compared, not on a general preference for one badge over the other. For mainly urban use on a tight budget, an entry-level version such as the MG ZS deserves to be compared with its Geely equivalent in the same segment and with the same powertrain. For use combining long trips and a need for power, the best-equipped Geely versions show, according to the comparisons reviewed, higher power output that may justify a higher budget.

In every case, the method stays the same: identify the body segment, the powertrain and the exact version on each side, compare written quotes covering equivalent configurations, then check the model's actual availability and the trade-in conditions for the current vehicle. A brand name does not replace this case-by-case verification.
