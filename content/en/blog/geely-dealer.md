Finding a **Geely dealer** in France involves three checks: does the location represent the brand, can it provide the required model, and how will it support the car afterwards? A network expansion announcement does not always answer all three. An announced site, an open showroom and an authorised workshop can describe different services.

In the Paris region, Como’s directory checked on 14 September 2026 explicitly names five Geely locations. Other distributors present the brand in several French regions. This guide provides documented reference points and a practical approach to visiting a dealer. It is not a ranking based on personal showroom visits.

## How can a French Geely location be identified?

Start with Geely Auto’s French website. It establishes the range intended for France and provides access to the brand’s contact arrangements. The distributor’s own website then supplies an address, contact details and a description of services.

Distinguish these from an ordinary vehicle advertisement. A business may offer a used or imported car without officially representing the manufacturer. The word Geely in an advert does not itself establish sales or after-sales authorisation.

The same caution applies to other brands within the group. A Volvo, smart or Zeekr dealer does not automatically become a Geely dealer. Teams, equipment and authorisations may differ. Our [Zeekr introduction]({{< relref "zeekr.md" >}}) explains the distinction between industrial ownership and commercial networks.

Before travelling, compare the distributor’s information with the manufacturer’s and contact the location. Confirmation should concern the specific model and service: viewing a car, taking a test drive, purchasing available stock or servicing an existing vehicle.

The exact address matters. A group can have neighbouring buildings or brand entrances. In Bastille, the Geely entry is listed at 242 rue de Bercy; replacing it with another Como address appearing in the same directory could send a visitor to the wrong entrance.

These five addresses describe the entries identified on the consulted page, not every development project within the group. Announced future sites are not proof that they have opened. Current operational information is more useful for an appointment than a dealership count in an older news story.

## Geely’s network also extends beyond Paris

Geely is not confined to the Paris region. Auto Réal presents a Geely dealership in Bordeaux. Elypse Autos has announced its participation in the network, and Baldassari Automobiles has a dedicated Geely page. These sources demonstrate the involvement of different groups without establishing an exhaustive national inventory.

For a regional search, work city by city. Verify the current address, contact options and available service. An article announcing a presence in an urban area may not specify the showroom entrance or the date from which test drives become possible.

Distance should be considered in two ways. An occasional journey to discover a car may be manageable; repeated servicing or diagnostic visits create a different requirement. The nearest sales location is not necessarily the most convenient workshop for home or work.

## Which models can be requested for a viewing or test?

The French catalogue checked presents the electric E2, electric E5 and Starray EM-i plug-in hybrid. Catalogue inclusion does not guarantee that every trim is immediately available in every showroom. The display vehicle may also differ from the test car.

When booking, specify the model, desired version and purpose of the test. Checking space for three rear passengers involves different needs from evaluating charging or faster-road driving. Ask whether the test car matches the version in the quotation.

## Sales, workshops and warranty are separate subjects

Sales staff explain configuration, availability and purchase or rental proposals. Workshops handle maintenance and diagnostic questions. Warranty cover comes from specific documents with a defined scope and conditions. A positive answer on one subject does not automatically settle the other two.

### Where will servicing take place?

Ask for the workshop address that actually supports the model. For an electrified vehicle, identify the operation: routine maintenance, a charging issue, battery diagnostics or body repair. Some work may be performed locally while other jobs go elsewhere.

A quoted parts lead time is not necessarily the total repair time. Diagnosis, possible authorisation, transport and workshop availability occur at different stages. Documented arrangements are more useful than a general promise of speed.

### What does the warranty cover?

Check the conditions applying to the actual car, especially if it is already registered or comes from another country. Identify the start date, remaining term, mileage restrictions and servicing obligations. Do not automatically transfer a foreign warranty to a French vehicle or vice versa.

For an extension or assistance package, establish the provider and what happens if the vehicle is immobilised. An advertised duration does not explain every service included.

## How can two dealers be compared beyond price?

Clarity is a useful first criterion. Are the quoted and demonstrated versions identified? Are fees itemised? Are the delivery location and workshop specified? These points can be established before any decision.

| Subject | Practical question |
|---|---|
| Test vehicle | Which version will be available, and when? |
| Order | Is this an identified stock car or a factory order? |
| Delivery | What timetable and documents are provided? |
| Servicing | Which address and contact support this model? |
| Warranty | Which conditions apply to this particular vehicle? |
| Services | What is included, optional or charged separately? |

Online reviews may offer signals, but their content matters. A review of another brand, another workshop or a previous team does not necessarily describe the current Geely experience. Repeated, specific observations are more informative than one overall rating.

## Preparing a visit that answers useful questions

Make a short list of requirements: mileage, journeys, passengers, parking and charging access. These help staff demonstrate meaningful differences between versions and keep the visit from focusing entirely on equipment that will rarely be used.

If loading is important, bring or measure the relevant objects. Check boot access, rear entry and driving position. Everyday controls and charging settings can be explored while stationary, leaving the drive itself for assessing the car on the road.

After the appointment, request a written quotation and documents resolving any unanswered points. The dealer can then be assessed through concrete evidence: consistent information, a vehicle suited to the intended use and clearly explained ongoing support.
