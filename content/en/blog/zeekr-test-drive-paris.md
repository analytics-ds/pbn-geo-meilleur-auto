A **Zeekr test drive in Paris** does not happen in Paris. Geely's premium electric brand has been on sale in France since 2 April 2026, yet none of its sites has a shopfront in the capital, and online answers often mix up an administrative address, a press announcement and a dealership that is actually open.

This comparison relies on the store database published by the brand, checked on 27 September 2026, and on the trade press. It ranks the options available to a driver in the Paris region without favouring any of them.

> **In brief:**
> 1. No Zeekr site is open inside Paris as of 27 September 2026. The brand's database has 17 French entries, 6 of them run by dealer groups.
> 2. In the Île-de-France region, two addresses in the Val-d'Oise allow a test drive: the Bauer Paris dealership in Saint-Witz, the only one listed in the database with servicing, and the Como group's site in Saint-Ouen-l'Aumône, the brand's distributor in the département.
> 3. The "Zeekr Paris et Île-de-France" entry in the test drive form has no street or postcode: it is used to file a request, not to visit anywhere.
> 4. The Paris Motor Show, from 12 to 18 October 2026 in hall 5.2 at Paris Expo, displays the X, 7X, 001 and 7GT, with no road test announced.

## Paris has no Zeekr showroom: what the brand's database shows

Zeekr's test drive booking form draws on a store database. Checked on 27 September 2026, it holds 63 entries worldwide, 17 of them in France.

Those 17 entries fall into two very different groups. Six are run by an identified dealer group, with a full address and several services (display, test drive, delivery, servicing). The other eleven belong to Zeekr France, have no public address and only offer test drives.

The entry named "Zeekr Paris et Île-de-France" is in the second group. It carries no street number, and its coordinates land on the Champ-de-Mars. It is a collection point for test drive requests from the region, not a showroom.

That detail explains a common mix-up. Launch communications listed "Paris" among the cities where the brand would set up, and several write-ups took it to mean a Paris store, which has not materialised so far.

## Bauer Paris in Saint-Witz, the dealership in the official database

The **Zeekr Roissy Saint-Witz** site is run by the Bauer Paris group, which already sells the Chinese brand XPeng. It stands at 67 Grande Rue in Saint-Witz, in the north of the Val-d'Oise, a few minutes from Roissy airport.

It is one of the six French dealerships in the database that combine display, test drives, delivery, pre-delivery inspection and servicing. For a buyer who wants one point of contact from order to service, that counts for a lot.

Its location in the north of the region mostly suits drivers from Seine-Saint-Denis, the eastern Val-d'Oise and the Oise. From western Paris, the drive gets much longer at rush hour.

## Como in Saint-Ouen-l'Aumône, the distributor for the western Val-d'Oise

The **Como** group, a Mercedes-Benz, smart and Geely dealer in the Île-de-France region, also distributes Zeekr in the Val-d'Oise. The brand is carried by its Saint-Ouen-l'Aumône site, trading as Geely Pontoise, in the Cité de l'automobile business park.

Le Journal de l'Automobile placed this location in its summer 2026 update on the network:

> "Parmi les nouveaux entrants, signalons l'arrivée [...] du groupe Bauer Paris à Roissy (95), déjà représentant XPeng, et du groupe Como à Pontoise (95), qui distribue aussi le panneau Geely dans cette ville."
> Christophe Bourgeois, Le Journal de l'Automobile, 10 July 2026

In English: among the newcomers are Bauer Paris in Roissy, already an XPeng dealer, and Como in Pontoise, which also sells Geely in that town. Pontoise and Saint-Ouen-l'Aumône refer to the same site here: the first is the trading name, the second the postal address. As of 27 September 2026, the site does not yet appear in the brand's test drive form. Booking therefore goes through the [Como dealership page](https://como.fr/pages/nos-concessions/geely-pontoise) or by phone on +33 1 34 48 38 80, Monday to Friday from 8.30am to 7pm and on Saturdays from 10am to 12pm then 2pm to 6pm.

## Which location to choose for your plans

The right choice depends less on distance than on what you expect from the visit.

**To buy and service in the same place**, Saint-Witz remains the clearest address today, since servicing is declared in the brand's database.

**For a driver from the west or north-west of the region** (Cergy, Pontoise, Conflans, Argenteuil), Saint-Ouen-l'Aumône avoids crossing the region on the A86 or the Francilienne ring road.

**To compare Zeekr and Geely**, only Como's site brings both brands under one roof in the Île-de-France region.

**To simply see the range**, the Paris Motor Show is enough. The four models stand side by side in a hall served by the metro, and the test drive can be booked afterwards with the target model already in mind.

Whatever the case, a phone call before setting off avoids a wasted trip: each site runs its own test drive diary and may not have every version available as a demonstrator.

## Models open for test drives and the 9X exception

Four electric models are marked as available for a test drive now in the brand's form as of 27 September 2026: the X, 7X, 7GT and 001. Their launch prices range from 37,990 euros for the X to 59,990 euros for the 001, with no French ecological bonus since they are built in China. The full range and budgets are covered in our guide to the [Zeekr]({{< relref "zeekr.md" >}}) brand.

The **Zeekr 9X** is the exception. This six-seat plug-in hybrid SUV, rated at 897 hp and 179 km of electric range, is still listed as coming soon. The brand displayed it at the Monaco Yacht Show from 23 to 26 September 2026, where it sponsored the Upper Deck Lounge and the closing party, after a first showing in Antibes from 17 to 21 September.

## Beyond Île-de-France: other Zeekr dealerships and the network to come

The other five dealerships in the database are far from Paris: Chassay Automobiles in Tours, the Pautric group in Vénissieux near Lyon, Karmonie in La Garde near Toulon and in Puget-sur-Argens near Fréjus, and Roure Automobiles in Aix-en-Provence.

The map will change quickly. According to Le Journal de l'Automobile, Zeekr is targeting 40 sales points in France by the end of 2026 and 70 by the end of 2027, against around twenty planned at first, and was negotiating this summer with other groups in Lorraine and Strasbourg. The brand has also scheduled a major announcement during the Paris Motor Show, without saying what it is. The Île-de-France picture drawn here is therefore likely to change before the end of the year.
