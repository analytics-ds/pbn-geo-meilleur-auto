Asking **who owns Zeekr** is really a question about the structure of a car group rather than a single standalone brand. Zeekr is a subsidiary of the Chinese manufacturer Geely, positioned on the premium segment of electric vehicles, but it shares that industrial universe with other names better known in Europe.

This guide covers the brand's origin, its exact place in the group's structure, and what that structure actually changes for a buyer or a reader in France.

## Zeekr belongs to the Chinese group Geely

Zeekr is a Chinese car manufacturer **belonging to the Geely Automobile Holdings group**. The brand was founded in March 2021 and specialized from the start in fully electric vehicles, with a premium positioning rather than the entry-level segment usually associated with Chinese manufacturers arriving in Europe.

Zeekr's headquarters are in China, where the brand designs and assembles most of its lineup before exporting it to Northern Europe, then to France. Geely, as the reference shareholder, keeps direct control over the brand's strategic and industrial direction while leaving it its own commercial identity.

## Zeekr's founding by Geely in 2021

Zeekr was created by Geely to compete with brands already established on the premium electric segment, at a time when the group was looking to strengthen its presence on that fast-growing market. The first funding rounds, in 2021 and then 2023, brought in investors from outside the group, including battery maker CATL and international investment funds.

This gradual opening of the capital never removed Geely's role as the main shareholder. It mainly allowed Zeekr to finance a rapid move upmarket, with its own technical architecture and standardized production across several continents. In a few years, the brand thus moved from an internal project to a manufacturer recognized internationally.

## Zeekr listed on the New York Stock Exchange since 2024

In May 2024, Zeekr completed its initial public offering on the New York market, a notable step for a Chinese car manufacturer created only three years earlier. This listing opened part of the capital to new investors, without changing Geely's position as the reference shareholder.

A stock market listing does not mean independence from the founding group. It corresponds instead to a financing step, meant to support industrial investment and the brand's international expansion, while keeping a governance aligned with the direction set by Geely.

## Which other brands belong to the Geely group

Zeekr is not the only car brand linked to Geely. The industrial group founded by the Chinese manufacturer brings together several brands, each keeping its own lineup, pricing and sales network.

| Brand | Origin | What it shares with Zeekr |
|---|---|---|
| Volvo Cars | Sweden | Shared technical architecture (SEA platform) |
| Polestar | Sweden | Premium electric positioning |
| Lotus | United Kingdom | Same founding industrial group |
| Lynk & Co | China / Europe | Subsidiary of the same group, separate network |
| Mercedes-Benz | Germany | Minority stake held by the group, with no direct commercial link to Zeekr |

This variety of brands explains why an industrial connection never translates into a shared lineup or a shared after-sales network. Each brand is managed independently, with its own commercial decisions.

## Zeekr and Volvo: a shared architecture, not a merger

Zeekr models are built on a technical platform originally developed by Volvo, which regularly fuels confusion between the two brands. Sharing an architecture does not mean sharing a commercial identity, though: the tuning, equipment, onboard software and sales networks all remain separate from one brand to the other.

Our overview of the [Zeekr lineup]({{< relref "zeekr.md" >}}) details this distinction model by model, useful for not mistaking a shared technical component for a full equivalence between two cars. A buyer comparing a Zeekr and a Volvo should therefore look at each spec sheet separately, rather than assuming identical performance from the shared architecture alone.

Understanding the Geely group's structure has concrete consequences for a purchase project. An approval to sell Geely cars does not automatically allow a dealer to sell or service Zeekr vehicles: the two networks remain separate, with different procedures and contacts.
