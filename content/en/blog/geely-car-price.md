On 14 September 2026, the displayed starting references were **€20,990 for the E2**, **€34,990 for the Starray EM-i** and **€37,990 for the E5**, before the options, fees and conditions specific to an individual quotation. These are entry-level references, not prices for every version.

Cash prices, launch promotions and monthly rentals need separate treatment. This guide concerns French-market vehicles and euro prices; it does not present UK offers. Its purpose is to make the different figures comparable without mixing markets or powertrains.

A commercial page can continue to display a promotion after one of its qualifying stages has ended. The reference retained is the €20,990 recommended price dated 1 September 2026 in the manufacturer’s terms. Any additional reduction needs to be supported by the conditions applicable to that customer’s order.

## E2, E5 and Starray EM-i: which price for which use?

The E2 is positioned as an electric city car. Before comparing trims, establish daily distance, parking requirements and available charging. A larger battery can provide flexibility, but its value depends on the actual pattern of journeys.

The E5 meets a requirement for an electric SUV. Its budget should be compared with other electric vehicles of similar size and equipment. Battery capacity and charging performance can matter more than cosmetic trim, particularly for regular motorway travel.

The Starray EM-i is a plug-in hybrid. Its practical appeal depends partly on being able to charge it regularly. Comparing its energy cost with a battery-electric car requires a distinction between miles driven on electricity and those involving the combustion engine. A homologated consumption figure does not recreate every driving situation.

## Why do online Geely prices vary so much?

The first reason is the country. Model names, equipment, fees and currencies differ across markets. Converting an overseas price into euros does not produce a French price. Even when the photographed vehicle looks identical, its specification and supporting services may differ.

The second reason is the vehicle’s status: a new factory order, demonstrator, registered stock vehicle or used car. Mileage, first registration and warranty start date then become part of the comparison. An attractive price for one example does not become the starting price of an entire range.

Websites are also updated at different times. One page may retain an older rental figure while another promotes a newer offer. Detailed conditions and a dated quotation are more reliable than an isolated amount in a short summary.

This is particularly relevant to [Geely Coolray prices]({{< relref "geely-coolray-price.md" >}}), which appear frequently on overseas websites. That guide separates markets and explains why the model should not automatically be added to the French catalogue.

## How should two quotations be compared?

Start by identifying the vehicle: model, trim, battery or engine, colour and options. Without a common specification, the price difference may simply reflect missing equipment. Availability also matters: an identified stock car and an order with a delivery lead time may not meet the same needs.

Next, separate the fees. Request a breakdown of options, services, registration, delivery and preparation. Any trade-in should remain identifiable: it concerns the outgoing car and should not obscure the purchase price of the replacement.

| Quotation item | Useful check |
|---|---|
| Version | Same trim, powertrain and battery |
| Equipment | Included options, accessories and services identified |
| Fees | Detailed amounts and explanation of optional or necessary items |
| Discount | Validity period, conditions and any finance requirement |
| Availability | Identified vehicle, stated timetable and delivery arrangements |
| Trade-in | Separate valuation and any reassessment conditions |

For a lease, add the term, mileage allowance, initial rental and included services. Return conditions also matter. A displayed monthly payment describes only part of the agreement, not every expense incurred during it.

This approach does not replace reading the contract. It prevents a misleading comparison between advertisements based on different assumptions.

## Purchase price is only part of the motoring budget

Insurance needs a quote for the exact version and driver profile. Tyre costs depend partly on wheel dimensions. Maintenance should follow the model’s own programme rather than assumptions drawn from another vehicle in the group.

An energy estimate starts with annual mileage and realistic use. For an electric car, separate home and public charging. For a plug-in hybrid, include how often it will actually be plugged in. Without these assumptions, a supposedly universal monthly running cost has little meaning.

Resale forecasts require similar caution. A recently introduced brand has less local history than a long-established range. Testing several future-value assumptions is more useful than promising a precise depreciation rate.
