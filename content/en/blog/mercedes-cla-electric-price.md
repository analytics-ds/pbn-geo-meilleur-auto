The price of a **Mercedes CLA Electric** cannot be summed up in a single figure. Since launch, the range has grown with a more accessible entry version, and the gap between the headline rate seen in advertising and the amount on an actually configured quote can be significant. Here are the recorded rates by version and trim, along with financing options, to size up a budget before comparing concrete offers.

## How much does a Mercedes CLA Electric cost today?

As of early December 2025, the electric CLA saloon range comprises three engines. The CLA 200 EQ, the most recent addition, now opens the range at a rate below that of the equivalent micro-hybrid version.

| Version | Power | Battery | Price from |
|---|---|---|---|
| CLA 200 EQ | 224 hp | 58 kWh | €48,050 |
| CLA 250+ | 272 hp | 85 kWh | €52,900 |
| CLA 350 4MATIC | 354 hp | 85 kWh | €62,150 |

These figures correspond to the lowest recorded rate per version, in entry-level trim. The trim level chosen can add several thousand euros to the bill, a point detailed further below.

## Three engines, three different budgets

### CLA 200 EQ, the most affordable version

The CLA 200 EQ develops 224 horsepower with a 58 kWh battery. It shows the lowest starting rate of the electric range, under the 50,000 euro mark in Progressive Line trim. It is also the version whose recorded WLTP range, 541 kilometres, remains the most modest of the trio.

### CLA 250+, the range-to-price balance

The CLA 250+ carries a 272 horsepower rear-wheel-drive setup and the largest battery in the range, 85 kWh. At launch, it already showed the best recorded ranges of the electric CLA line-up, beyond 790 kilometres on the WLTP cycle depending on trim and wheel choice.

### CLA 350 4MATIC, the all-wheel-drive version

The CLA 350 4MATIC adds all-wheel drive and raises power to 354 horsepower, for a starting rate above 62,000 euros. It shares the 85 kWh battery of the CLA 250+, with a slightly lower WLTP range due to the added weight and drivetrain.

## How much does trim level change the price?

Each engine comes in several trim levels, from the most basic to the most complete. On the CLA 350 4MATIC for instance, the recorded gap between the entry trim and the fullest trim exceeds 5,000 euros, without any change to power or battery capacity.

A significant share of the equipment remains optional, from dual-zone automatic climate control to flush door handles and the wireless smartphone charger. Building a configuration close to what is seen in advertising can therefore add several thousand euros to the headline price. Comparing two quotes requires checking that the equipment list is genuinely identical, not just the trim name.

## Range and consumption: what the price includes

Recorded WLTP range spans from 541 kilometres for the CLA 200 EQ to over 790 kilometres for certain CLA 250+ configurations. Advertised electricity consumption sits between roughly 12 and 15 kWh/100 km on the combined cycle depending on version and trim, with lower figures on the urban cycle.

This range is not determined by battery size alone: wheels, trim and the associated aerodynamics noticeably change the final figure for the same engine. A higher budget therefore does not automatically guarantee the longest range, which makes a close reading of the exact configuration's technical sheet essential before comparing a price. ## LOA or LLD: how to read a financing offer

Lease-to-own (LOA) and long-term leasing (LLD) remain the two most common formulas offered for this type of premium saloon. One dealer offer recorded for a CLA 200 in AMG Line trim, for example, mentioned a first payment of 780 euros followed by 36 monthly payments of the same amount. This kind of figure depends directly on the deposit paid, the term chosen and the contracted annual mileage: it cannot be transposed as-is from one offer to another.

Before comparing two proposals, it is worth checking the exact term, the mileage included, the deposit amount, and, for an LOA, the buy-back value set at the end of the contract. A lower monthly payment can hide a higher upfront deposit or a restricted annual mileage. The comparison method applied to the Chinese car market in the guide on [Geely Coolray prices]({{< relref "geely-coolray-price.md" >}}) still holds here: only compare written proposals covering a strictly identical configuration.

## Buying a used CLA Electric: a market still in its infancy

Because the model is recent, the used market remains limited at this stage in France. The few listings available mostly concern former demonstration cars or short-term rental vehicles, not yet a volume of private resales comparable to older models. A discount on such a recent model should be examined with caution: it can signal high mileage, an incomplete service history, or simply a first owner in a hurry to sell.

For a purchase through the dealer network, questions of availability, delivery lead time and after-sales follow-up are detailed in the guide on [buying a CLA Electric in France]({{< relref "buy-mercedes-cla-electric-france.md" >}}), useful to complement a price-only reading.

## Questions to ask before signing a quote or a financing contract

Before signing, it remains worth getting written confirmation of: the full list of equipment included in the displayed price, the delivery lead time actually committed to, the total amount due in case of early exit from a leasing contract, and the exact terms of the buy-back value for an LOA. These details do not always appear in an online simulation and deserve written confirmation from the dealer before placing an order.
