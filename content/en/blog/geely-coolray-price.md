A search for **Geely Coolray price** returns several countries, trims and sometimes used cars. **There is no single Coolray price that applies everywhere.** On 14 September 2026, Automobile.tn’s model page displayed a starting figure of **72,800 Tunisian dinars**. That concerns an offer presented for Tunisia, not a vehicle delivered and registered in France.

The French Geely and Como pages checked highlighted E2, E5 and Starray EM-i. We did not identify an official French Coolray price on those pages. This does not establish that no import advertisements exist. It means that an overseas figure should not be presented as a French list price. This guide addresses research from France, not a UK sales offer.

## Which Coolray price can actually be compared?

First identify the offer’s market. Website language is not enough: a French-language page may concern Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco or an exporter elsewhere. Currency, seller address and delivery conditions establish the context.

| Reference checked | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Automobile.tn Coolray page | Displayed starting figure of TND 72,800 for Tunisia | French price, import budget or French availability |
| Geely’s global New Coolray page | International model presentation and specifications | Local price or identical equipment in every country |
| Geely France and Como pages | French catalogue presented when checked | An official French Coolray price, not identified there |

Overseas prices help explain local positioning. They do not by themselves establish the budget for a buyer in France. Version, seller, services and handover location must be known before a meaningful comparison can be calculated.

## Which vehicle does the Coolray name describe?

Geely’s international New Coolray page describes a petrol SUV with a 1.5-litre turbo engine and seven-speed dual-clutch transmission. It lists a length of 4,380 mm. The manufacturer explains that pictures and specifications are indicative and that the actual vehicle depends on its country or region.

That qualification matters. A recent photograph may accompany an advertisement for another model year, and trim names can vary between markets. The Coolray name alone therefore cannot establish exact power, equipment, software or configuration.

### Model year and first registration are different facts

A car first registered in 2026 may belong to an earlier model year or stock batch. Request the model year, production date where available and first registration. For a used vehicle, add mileage, service history and provenance.

### Check a trim equipment item by equipment item

A panoramic roof, upholstery, driver assistance and cameras are not necessarily identical on every version. A written equipment list is more useful than treating a promotional photograph as proof. ## Why do online amounts differ so widely?

Some results describe a locally distributed new car. Others concern a used vehicle, a temporary promotion or an export transaction. An export price may relate to one stage of the transaction rather than a car ready for use in its destination country.

Currency can also be misunderstood. Amounts marked DT, DH or DA are not euro prices. Even an accurate exchange-rate conversion does not recreate the fees, procedures or services attached to the car.

The scope of the price is another source of variation: options, transport, preparation, commercial warranty, registration or intermediary services. The seller should identify included and excluded items. Without that breakdown, two amounts cover unknown scopes.

Search results can retain an old price after a page changes. Open the listing, check its date and request confirmation. The amount cited here remains a dated reference, not a promise of current stock availability.

## Why not simply convert a foreign price into euros?

A currency conversion answers a limited question: what is the equivalent value of an amount? It does not answer the vehicle question: what does this example cost, in this specification, with the documents and services needed for the intended country of use?

Before considering a cross-border purchase, identify the parties and services. Who sells the car? Who arranges transport? Where does handover occur? What documents are supplied? Who handles the remaining procedures? Answers should concern the actual vehicle, not another example used to illustrate an advertisement.

The final budget should not be estimated by adding a universal allowance to the overseas price. It depends on the vehicle’s route and the costs actually applicable. Registration feasibility and corresponding requirements need confirmation from the competent services before any commitment.

A detailed offer is not automatically the best offer, but it can be examined. An amount accompanied by a few photographs leaves too many unanswered questions for a serious comparison.

## Coolray servicing and warranty: what needs clarification?

A Geely network in France does not establish support for every international version. The model, origin, parts requirements and warranty conditions need explicit discussion with the workshop concerned.

Ask a concrete question: can this establishment service and diagnose this particular car? Does it have the necessary documentation and equipment? Which operations can be performed locally? A general statement about the brand does not confirm support for a vehicle outside the local range.

For warranty cover, identify the organisation making the commitment, covered territory, remaining term and mileage. A seller’s warranty may differ from manufacturer cover. Exclusions, maintenance obligations and the process after a breakdown deserve a complete reading.

## What should be requested to compare two advertisements?

A comparison sheet can remain short while being precise. The aim is to collect the same information for both vehicles. An empty field is a question to ask, not a fact to guess.

| Information | Detail to request |
|---|---|
| Vehicle | Exact version, model year, engine and transmission |
| History | First registration, mileage and servicing |
| Price | Currency, validity and included or excluded costs |
| Delivery | Location, timetable and agreed services |
| Documents | List of papers supplied for the specific vehicle |
| Warranty | Provider, territory, duration, mileage and exclusions |
| After-sales | Workshop that has confirmed model support |

A used car’s condition needs assessment on the vehicle itself. Pictures do not reveal every repair, wear item or equipment fault. An independent inspection and maintenance documents add evidence beyond the seller’s description.

Separating these details helps explain a seemingly attractive offer. A lower amount can reflect a different version, history or package of services. Only after clarification does the price difference become interpretable.

## Which French-range alternatives might be considered?

Someone interested in the Coolray may primarily want a compact SUV, automatic transmission or a particular budget. Reframe those requirements before expanding the shortlist. An electric car and a petrol SUV do not meet exactly the same needs when regular charging is unavailable.

The E5 and Starray EM-i are presented in Como’s Geely range. The first is electric and the second a plug-in hybrid, so neither is a direct equivalent of a petrol Coolray. They can still enter a comparison when size, budget and charging arrangements suit the intended use.
