Buying a **Zeekr** in France raises different questions from simply discovering the brand: where to find a dealer, which model fits which use, what budget to plan, and in what order to move before signing. The premium electric brand from the Geely group has opened its first dealer sites in France, but its network is still young and unevenly spread.

This guide gathers the useful reference points for a purchase project, new or used, drawn from dated and verifiable editorial sources. It does not replace a test drive or a written quote from the chosen dealer.

## Buying a Zeekr in France: what this actually means

The Zeekr brand belongs to the Geely group's industrial world, but it keeps its own range, its own sales network and its own pricing. A purchase project therefore has to be reasoned around the exact Zeekr model, in its exact version, rather than around a group-level comparison. Our overview of the [Zeekr range]({{< relref "zeekr.md" >}}) details this distinction and how to read spec sheets by version.

Buying an imported new car from a recently established brand calls for particular care on three points: real availability of the model sought, the delay between ordering and delivery, and how after-sales service is organised near home. These three elements vary more for a brand still setting up than for a generalist manufacturer already established for a long time.

## Which Zeekr models can be bought in France?

Four models make up the range sold in France: the compact crossover X, the family SUV 7X, the sporty estate 7GT and the large estate 001. Each answers a different use and a different budget.

| Model | Body style | Main use |
|---|---|---|
| Zeekr X | Compact crossover | Urban and suburban trips |
| Zeekr 7X | Family SUV | Family use and large luggage |
| Zeekr 7GT | Sporty estate | Road use and style, smaller boot |
| Zeekr 001 | Large estate | Long trips and loading |

### The Zeekr X, the entry point of the range

The X is the most accessible model in the range. Its compact crossover size makes urban parking easier, but range and power vary significantly depending on the version chosen. Comparing two listings therefore means checking the exact version, not just the model name.

### The Zeekr 7X and the Zeekr 7GT, two different profiles

The 7X is an SUV built for family use, with a large boot and a raised driving position. The 7GT shares the same technical base but adopts a lower estate silhouette, more road oriented, at the cost of a smaller boot volume for an equivalent trim. Choosing between the two depends more on daily use than on exterior style alone.

### The Zeekr 001, the brand's historical model

The 001 made the brand known before its arrival in France. It remains the longest model in the range and the best equipped as standard. Its large estate format means checking garage ceiling height and the usual parking space width before any purchase.

## What dealer network exists for buying a Zeekr?

Zeekr's French network was built up gradually from 2026, with development deliberately independent from that of Volvo or Polestar, two other Geely group brands already present in France. The first dealer sites opened in Aix-en-Provence, before further locations opened in Lyon, Fréjus, Saint-Étienne and Rennes.

The stated objective for 2026 was twenty-five to thirty dealer sites, with an after-sales network twice as dense, intended to double the following year. This footprint therefore remains tighter than that of a generalist manufacturer. Before travelling, checking that the nearest dealer actually carries the model sought, not just the range as a whole, avoids a wasted trip.

## What budget should be planned for a new purchase?

French pricing recorded in June 2026 gives an order of magnitude per model, before options and additional fees.

| Model | Entry version | Indicative base price |
|---|---|---|
| Zeekr X | Core RWD | 37,990 euros |
| Zeekr 7GT | Core RWD | 45,990 euros |
| Zeekr 7X | Core RWD | 52,990 euros |
| Zeekr 001 | Long Range RWD | 59,990 euros |

These amounts correspond to the entry version of each model and do not presume the price of a higher trim. Since Zeekr vehicles are assembled in China, they are not eligible for certain aid schemes reserved for vehicles meeting a French environmental score, which should be factored into the final budget. The amount compared between two quotes should always include registration fees and any options already fitted.

## Buying a new or used Zeekr: what are the practical differences?

The used Zeekr market remains, by construction, small in France: the brand has only been sold there for a short time, and the number of vehicles already resold is mechanically limited. A used purchase therefore calls for closer checks on service history, mileage and battery condition, given the limited track record of these models on French roads.

Conversely, a new purchase allows choosing the version, colour and options, but involves a delay between ordering and delivery that can vary depending on model availability at the time of order. ## What steps to follow between ordering and delivery?

Before signing an order form, several checks limit unpleasant surprises. Confirming in writing the exact version, the options included and the total price avoids any discrepancy with the initial listing. Asking for the stated delivery timeframe, not an optimistic range, allows anticipating a possible extra wait typical of a brand still setting up.

Next, checking the terms of the manufacturer warranty, its duration, mileage limit and covered components, as well as how breakdowns are handled far from the original dealer, matters. A still tight after-sales network makes this question particularly relevant for Zeekr: the site used for the purchase is not necessarily the one that will carry out routine servicing in later years.

Finally, a test drive representative of the usual route, in slow traffic as well as on faster roads, remains the only way to confirm that a spec sheet matches real use. No figure, however precise, replaces this personal check before a financial commitment of this size.
