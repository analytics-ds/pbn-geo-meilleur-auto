## A view of the automotive world

Meilleur Auto is an online automotive publication. Models and comparisons, electric mobility, industry economics, distribution networks and everyday motoring define its editorial scope.

The journal brings together published articles. Topics offer ways to explore, archives organise publications over time and author pages bring together each byline’s contributions.

## Who publishes the site?

Meilleur Auto is designed and controlled by datashake as part of its work with Como. This relationship is disclosed to put its editorial direction and content in context.

## An identifiable editorial team

The first articles will be signed by the Meilleur Auto editorial team, a collective byline. Figures and technical claims must be sourced. Road tests, testimonials and journalist biographies must not be invented.
